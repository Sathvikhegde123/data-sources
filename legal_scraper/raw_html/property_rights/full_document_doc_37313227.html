Satya Narain Agarwal vs. ACIT The assessee filed all those documents before the ld. CIT(A) who in turned forwarded the same to AO. These documents are registered documents and therefore, the same cannot be considered as after thought when the revenue did not controvert the contention of the assessee that the said cash was not included in the cash book filed by the assessee. Before us the assessee in furtherance to the evidence already on record also filed an affidavit wherein it was submitted that the said cash received was available and was not entered into the cash book. He also declared that the opening balance shown in the cash was also taken as incorrect and that fact was supported by the affidavit and that of the ITR of the previous placed on record. Considering both these evidence if that is corrected there is no negative balance for an amount of Rs. 4,86,143/- and therefore, we are of the considered view that since the cash generated by the assessee from those registered deed with that of the corrected opening cash on hand cannot be ignored and were not after thought. Based on these observations we see no reason to sustain that addition and thereby ground no. 1 raised by the assessee is allowed.

Ground no. 2 being general and the same is not required to be adjudicated.
