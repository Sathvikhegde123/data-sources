JUDGMENT

1. Plaintiff has filed suit for cancellation, declaration and permanent injunction.

2. It is the case of the plaintiff that she is Bhumidar in possession of land bearing khasra no. 43/12(3-17); 13(4-16); 14 (4-

16); 15(1-14); 17(3-19); 18(4-16); 19 (5-03); 23/1 (3 -08) and 43/28 (0-3) in all measuring 32 bighas and 12 biswas situated within the revenue estate of Ghuman Hera, Delhi, [hereinafter to called as "Suit Land"]

3. It is the case of the plaintiff that subject matter of the dispute pertains to the land comprising in Khasra no. 43/14 (4-16) and information was received that defendant no.1 has executed a sale deed in favour of defendant no.2 on 14.11.1996 purporting to be acted as General Attorney of plaintiff in respect of land comprising in Khasra no. 43/14 (4-16), [hereinafter to be called as "Land in Dispute"]

4. It is also averred that the power of attorney was registered in the office of Sub Registrar office on 19.07.1996 and sale deed being CS No.612639/16 Lakshmi Devi Vs. Gajender Singh and Ors. Page 2 / 45 document No.14619 in Addl Book No.1, Volume No. 8603 at page 9 to 17 was executed on 14.11.1996 by defendant no.1 in favour of defendant no.2 who was an Ex Tehsildar of Mahrauli Tehsil.

5. It is further averred that land bearing khasra no. 43/12( 3-

17); 13(4-16); 14 (4-16); 15(1-4); 17(3-19); 18(4-16); 19 (5-03); 23/1 (3 -08) and 43/28 (0-3) in all measuring 32 bighas and 12 biswas situated within the revenue estate of Ghuman Hera, Delhi was alienated by the defendant No. 1 through separate sale deed and in this respect plaintiff was advised to file separate suits in respect of portion of land as mentioned in suit land in different sale deed.

6. It is also averred that on the basis of the sale deed the defendant no 1 and 2 have got the mutation in respect of the land without the knowledge of plaintiff. Mutation entries in respect of different sale deeds were sanctioned on 13.01.1997. It is averred that plaintiff did not receive single penny as consideration from any of the defendants.

7. It is also stated that at no stage of time plaintiff had contemplated to sell/ alienate her land through the agency of defendant no 1 or anybody else and it was also averred that defendant no 1 was never appointed as power of attorney on behalf of plaintiff.

8. It is further averred that plaintiff came to know through her CS No.612639/16 Lakshmi Devi Vs. Gajender Singh and Ors. Page 3 / 45 husband in the last week of September 1997 that the land as referred above was sold by the defendant No. 1 in favour of defendant no. 2 and entries were also made in the revenue record.

9. It is also averred that when husband of plaintiff became aware about the factum of mutation, he(husband of plaintiff) inspected the relevant revenue record and informed the plaintiff that mutation order in respect of the land in question has been passed and after getting information with to mutation of "suit land", an appeal was filed in respect of grant of sanction of mutation, appeal was before Deputy Commissioner, Kapashera and same is still pending. The plaintiff was also advised to file a suit for declaration, cancellation of the deed of attorney and sale deed on the basis of which the name of the defendant no 2 stands substituted as Bhumidar of plaintiff's land as mentioned in para no. 1 of the plaint.

10. Suit has been filed to get the sale deed as null and void and for setting aside mutation order dated 13.01.1997 as null and void ab initio.

11. It is pleaded that plaintiff is a simple illiterate housewife and has nothing to do with the worldly affair and defendant no 1 was treated like a son being the younger brother of husband and she never had any intention to appoint Defendant No.1 as her power of attorney. It is also stated that the contents of General Power of Attorney (GPA) CS No.612639/16 Lakshmi Devi Vs. Gajender Singh and Ors. Page 4 / 45 were not explained to defendant no.1 as she is not conversant with the English and signature appearing on the document were procured under the impression as if she is filing an application for sanction of mutation in respect of suit land. It is also stated that plaintiff did not put her thumb mark on deed of attorney under the impression that she is putting the same on the application for sanction of mutation of her land purchased in April 1996.

12. It is also averred that plaintiff was deceived by defendant no 1 as regards to actual nature and contents of deed of attorney and therefore, the alleged deed of attorney executed by plaintiff in favour of defendant no 1 is nullity and not binding on the plaintiff.

13. It is also averred that defendant no 3 was impleaded as party to the suit on the ground as per the recitals in the sale deed is that plaintiff had entered into an agreement for sale with confirming party vide agreement to sell dated 24.07.1996 and the plaintiff has received a consideration of Rs 495000/-. It is also asserted that no agreement to sell was entered by plaintiff with defendant no 3 or anyone else. It is also averred that defendant no 3 is totally unknown to the plaintiff. Plaintiff has also casted doubt on the recitals made in the sale deed.

14. An objection was also raised by the plaintiff that mutation entry was wrongly made by Tehseeldar since no notice was served on the plaintiff and it was submitted that the entire proceedings were CS No.612639/16 Lakshmi Devi Vs. Gajender Singh and Ors. Page 5 / 45 done at the instance of defendant no 2 without inviting any objection on plaintiff.

15. It is also averred that proclamation was not made in terms of mandatory provision of Delhi Land Revenue Act. The plaintiff has also challenged the proclamation issued under the signature of Tehsildar on 27.12.1996.

16. It is further averred that plaintiff was totally kept in dark with regard to the mutation proceedings due to which she could not file her objection. It is also pleaded that the order of mutation was illegal, void and without jurisdiction and liable to be set aside and declared as null and void.

17. It is also stated that plaintiff is in possession of the land in dispute although name of defendant no 2 is recorded as Bhumidar in the column no. 3 of Khasra Girdawari. It is also averred that on the basis of wrong entries in the revenue record showing defendant no 2 in possession and defendant no.2 is threatening to take the possession of the plaintiff over the suit land.

18. The plaintiff on the basis of aforesaid facts prayed for cancellation of sale deed and also for declaring the mutation dated 13.01.1997 in respect of khasra no. 43/14 measuring 4 bighas 16 biswas in case no. 861/TNG/96-97 sanctioning mutation in the name CS No.612639/16 Lakshmi Devi Vs. Gajender Singh and Ors. Page 6 / 45 of defendant no 2 as not binding on the plaintiff and for declaration it be declared as null and void.

19. The plaintiff has also prayed for the decree of cancellation, of Sale Deed executed in favour of defendant no.2 by defendant no.1. Plaintiff also prayed for decree of permanent injunction for restraining defendant no 2 from creating third party right in respect of subject matter of dispute and for interfering in the peaceful possession of plaintiff.

Therefore, this issue is decided in favour of plaintiff as against defendants.

80. Relief.

In view of the finding recorded on issue no.1 to 4, the suit filed by plaintiff is dismissed. No order as to cost.

Decree sheet be prepared.

File be consigned to record room as per rules.
