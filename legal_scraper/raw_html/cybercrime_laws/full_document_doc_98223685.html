6. Learned counsel for the complainant has not disputed the submissions made by the counsel opposite qua the parties having resolved their disputes. Learned counsel for the complainant also does not oppose the prayer made for granting the concession of bail to the petitioners.

8. In the facts and circumstances as enumerated hereinabove, the instant petitions are allowed. The petitioners be admitted to bail to the satisfaction of the trial Court/Duty Magistrate concerned. However, it is made clear that anything observed hereinabove shall not be construed to be an expression of opinion on the merits of the case.
