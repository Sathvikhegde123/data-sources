Madhya Pradesh High Court

Vijay Tripathi vs The State Of Madhya Pradesh on 24 April, 2015

     M.Cr.C. No. 12383/2014, 13902/2014, 15113/2014,
     15331/2014, 16017/2014, 19023/2014, 19025/2014,
                19027/2014 & 4843/2015
24.04.2015

M.Cr.C. No. 12383/2014, 13902/2014

      Shri Ajay Mishra, Senior Advocate with Shri Gaurav
Tiwari, Advocate for the applicant/Om Prakash Shukla.

      Shri P.K. Kaurav, Additional Advocate General and Shri
Prakash Gupta, Panel Lawyer for the respondent/State.

Counsel for the applicant submits that very recently the applicant has been denied of medical facilities, hence intends to file application for issuing appropriate direction to the State for providing medical facilities to the applicant during the hearing of the applications.

We have permitted the applicant to file the said application in Court. The same be numbered and listed alongwith main matters. As we have to defer the hearing for two reasons; firstly the State wants time to take instructions with reference to the factual position stated in the fresh application filed by the applicant and secondly as the applicant has filed two more bail applications (M.Cr.C. Nos.6623/2015 and 6624/2015) concerning other crimes which will have to be heard together and further the applicant intends to file two more bail applications in respect of two more crimes where he has been named as accused. In other words, six bail applications of this applicant will have to be considered together for which hearing is deferred till 01.05.2015. We permit the applicant to file bail applications in other crimes directly before this Court in the peculiar fact situation of the case as other applications in respect of other crimes of this applicant are pending consideration before this Court.

M.Cr.C. Nos. 15113/2014, 15331/2014, 16017/2014 & 4843/2015, Shri H.S. Chhabra, Advocate for the applicant/Laxmikant Sharma.

Shri P.K. Kaurav, Additional Advocate General and Shri Prakash Gupta, Panel Lawyer for the respondent/State.

List on 01.05.2015 alongwith M.Cr.C. No.12383/2014, as requested by the applicants.

M.Cr.C. Nos. 19023/2014, 19025/2014, 19027/2014 Dr. Anuvad Shrivastava, Advocate for the applicant/Vijay Tripathi.

Shri P.K. Kaurav, Additional Advocate General and Shri Prakash Gupta, Panel Lawyer for the respondent/State.

Heard counsel for the parties.

These bail applications have been filed by the applicant Vijay Tripathi, who has been named as accused in connection with Crime No.15/2013, 21/2013 and 16/2013 respectively. Crime No.15/2013 has been registered with Police Station S.T.F., Bhopal - commonly known as VYAPAM Examination Scam cases - for the offence punishable under Sections 409, 417/34, 420, 467, 468, 471, 201 and 120-B of IPC and under Section 65, 66 of Information and Technology Act and also under Section 3(Gha) 1, 2/4 of the M.P. Manyata Prapt Pariksha Adhiniyam, 1937 and Section 13(1)(d), 13(2) of the Prevention of Corruption Act; Crime No.21/2013 has been registered with Police Station S.T.F., Bhopal - for the offence punishable under Sections 420, 467, 468, 471 and 120-B of IPC and under Section 3(Gha) 1, 2/4 of the M.P. Manyata Prapt Pariksha Adhiniyam, 1937; Crime No.16/2013 has been registered with Police Station S.T.F., Bhopal - for the offence punishable under Sections 420, 467, 468, 471 and 120-B of IPC and under Section 65, 66 of Information and Technology Act and also under Section 3(Gha) 1, 2/4 of the M.P. Manyata Prapt Pariksha Adhiniyam, 1937 and Section 13(1)(d), 13(2) of the Prevention of Corruption Act.

The role ascribed to the applicant by the prosecution is that of a middleman. He had interacted with one Vijay Singh and O.P. Shukla. O.P. Shukla was the Officer on Special Duty of the then Minister Laxmikant Sharma. Vijay Singh was another middleman who had interacted with the beneficiaries. It is not in dispute that Vijay Singh has already been released on bail. All beneficiaries linked to Vijay Singh and in particular present applicant Vijay Tripathi have been arrested and released on bail. O.P.Shukla is already in jail in connection with Crime No.21/2013 and 16/2013 respectively besides other crime registered against him.

It is, fairly, stated by the learned counsel for the State, on instructions, that since charge-sheet is already filed in all the three crimes against the applicant and moreso final charge- sheet has been filed in Crime No.16/2013 and that investigation against the applicant is complete in all respects in all the crimes and further custodial interrogation of the applicant may not be necessary in those crimes, coupled with the fact that the applicant has not been named as accused in any other crime which factual position has been ascertained after thorough investigation and that the applicant through counsel undertakes to appear before the Investigating Officer as and when called for further investigation in respect of each of these crimes including against him as also to punctually appear before the trial Court to ensure early disposal of the criminal trials.

In the interest of justice, taking overall view of the matter, we deem it appropriate to grant bail to the applicant on following strict conditions:-

(1) The applicant shall furnish personal bond in the sum of Rs.2,00,000/- (Rupees Two Lac) in each crime with two solvent local sureties in the like amount to the satisfaction of the Trial Court for his regular appearance during the trial in connection with Crime Nos.15/2013, 21/2013 and 16/2013 respectively in Police Station S.T.F. Bhopal;
(2) The applicant shall also comply with the conditions enumerated under Section 437 (3) of Cr.P.C. meticulously;
(3) Additionally, the applicant will report to the nearest Police Station where he would reside at Gulmohar Colony, Bhopal (M.P.) once a week on every Sunday between 10:00 a.m. to 12:00 noon unless required to attend the office of the Investigating Officer at Bhopal on that day; and (4) The applicant will deposit his passport, if available, with the Investigating Agency, else file affidavit in this Court declaring that the applicant does not have any passport of any country. This compliance will be condition precedent for release on bail.

The bail applications stand disposed of accordingly. Certified copy as per rules.

                                                
         (A.M. Khanwilkar)                                    (Shantanu Kemkar)
           Chief Justice                                           Judge
