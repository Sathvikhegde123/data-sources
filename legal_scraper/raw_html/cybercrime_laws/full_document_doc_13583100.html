Clause (g) of section 43 adds a new dimension by extending the liability to pay damages by way of compensation even to those who provide "any assistance to any person to facilitate access to a computer ete. in contravention of the provisions of this Act, cules or regulations made thereunder." This is in addition to clause (a) which by itself makes unauthorized access unlawful so as to attract damages by way of compensation.
